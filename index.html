About Me

Passionate about technology, development, and solving real-world problems.

I'm Pranesh Saravanan, a Computer Science and Engineering student at Jeppiaar Engineering College, passionate about technology, web development, and creating modern digital experiences.

I'm currently developing my skills in Full Stack Development, with a focus on HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js, Python, databases, and REST APIs. I enjoy turning ideas into functional, responsive, and user-friendly applications while continuously learning new technologies.

Along with development, I have hands-on experience in application and website testing, bug reporting, and Zoho CRM, which has helped me understand software from both a developer and user perspective.

I'm particularly interested in UI/UX design, clean interfaces, problem-solving, and building applications that are simple and effective.

I consider myself a curious learner, hardworking developer, and problem solver who enjoys exploring new technologies and taking on challenging projects.

My goal is to grow as a Full Stack Developer and build impactful software that solves real-world problems.
